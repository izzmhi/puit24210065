Data Communication

Summary of Data Communication

Data communication refers to the exchange of information between devices through communication channels. It enables computers, smartphones, servers, and networks to transmit and receive data efficiently. Data communication is important in education, healthcare, banking, online learning, businesses, and social media.

Transmission Media

Transmission media are channels used to carry data signals. Examples include twisted pair cables, fibre optics, radio waves, and microwaves.

Network Devices

Routers, switches, modems, hubs, and access points help devices communicate and share information within networks.

Communication Models

The OSI and TCP/IP models explain how information moves from sender to receiver through communication layers.
